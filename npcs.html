The Baron's House

Baron Maxim Von Basten

Race: Unknown · Home: Abrams Rest

Rules the Blighted West with an iron fist from Abrams Rest, the region's only source of clean water. Traps citizens in indentured servitude: wages taxed heavily, quotas that accrue debt, and no leaving Abrams Rest until the debt is paid.

Selene Von Basten

Daughter of the Baron

Wears a silken coat, thigh-high boots, a silk face covering, layered jewellery, and a headdress with two blue tassels — and carries a large rifle. Encountered at the Riad, where she refused to enter herself and threatened the guards over the delay.

Lorcend Von Basten

Son of the Baron

Long silver hair, a scar on his cheek. Takes instructions from the Professor and brings him experiment subjects — and personally redirected Jareth's body toward the library the night the Silent Knives fell.

Believed to be the silver-haired enforcer who killed Old Bull and broke Hylanku's horn.

Professor Godt

"The brains to the Baron's brawn"

Experiments on creatures, magic users, and things pulled from the Drop. Mining has pushed closer to the Darklands since his arrival — and he's rumoured never to be seen by anyone recently.
