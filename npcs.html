Baron Maxim Von Basten

Race: Unknown · Home: Abrams Rest
  • Rules the Blighted West with an iron fist from Abrams Rest, the region's only source of clean water.
  • Traps citizens in indentured servitude: wages taxed heavily, quotas that accrue debt, and no leaving Abrams Rest until the debt is paid.
