The Baron's House

Baron Maxim Von Basten

Not yet met in person

Rules the Blighted West with an iron fist from Abrams Rest, the region's only source of clean water. Traps citizens in indentured servitude: wages taxed heavily, quotas that accrue debt, and no leaving Abrams Rest until the debt is paid.

Long silver hair, a scar on his cheek. Takes instructions from the Professor and brings him experiment subjects — and personally redirected Jareth's body toward the library the night the Silent Knives fell.

Believed to be the silver-haired enforcer who killed Old Bull and broke Hylanku's horn.

The Missing and the Dead

Callow May

Not yet met — known only through the Professor's journals

A green hag, and — as Coren has finally admitted to the party — his mother. Her son loved Jareth; when Coren left him for dead, she cursed him to never die and never heal rather than let him go. She made contact with the Professor over the body, allowed him to experiment freely, and asked only to be told if anyone ever came looking.

Coren VaelJarethProfessor Godt

Foxfire

Not yet met — known through Hylanku's past and secondhand reports

A young red-haired half-elven mage who kept a wandering caravan fed and watered with magic, staying hidden from the Baron's collars. Became Hylanku's closest friend during his years with the caravan.

When the Baron's men attacked to take her, Old Bull died protecting her and Hylanku was dragged away in chains — he never learned whether she got free. Years later, a servant in the Galleries recalled a red-haired mage matching her description passing through the slave auctions, bought by someone close to the Baron: short brown hair, brown eyes, never without a cigarette. Hylanku is still looking.

Jareth

Not yet met — known through Coren's account and the Professor's journals

Coren's former partner in the Silent Knives. On the night of the failed coup, Jareth never showed for the job — lost in a spell — and Coren found him back at the safehouse and stabbed him through the neck mid-casting before fleeing the city.

He should have died. A green hag named Callow May, whose son loved him, cursed him instead to never die and never heal. Recovered by the Professor as Subject 442, he's been altered piece by piece ever since — parts of him replaced with larger creatures, and dragon hide.

Old Bull

Encountered: The Coccium Ruins, years before the party's arrival (told in Session 10)

An elderly minotaur devoted to Jarak, the six-horned first minotaur — attuned to the earth and its spirits, and the one who kept a wandering caravan clear of danger for years. Found Hylanku half-feral among the Coccium Ruins, calmed him, and talked the caravan into taking him in. Spent years teaching him Jarak's faith, minotaur history, and the Petran nature of their people.

Stood against the Baron's men when they came to enslave Foxfire, and was beaten to his knees for it. A silver-haired enforcer — since identified as Lorcend Von Basten — shot him dead with the same bullet that shattered Hylanku's horn.

His body didn't stay buried. Recovered as Subject 469, stripped to the bone and bound with necromantic magic into one of the Professor's guardians.
