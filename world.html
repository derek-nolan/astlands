The Astlands, One Thousand Years On

The World

Locations, factions, races, and the things that live out past the city walls.

Locations

The Astlands

The name given to the world itself.

The Blighted West

A rocky, arid desert not unlike the Sonoran Desert — but the land itself is diseased. No plant life survives apart from twisted roots and dangerous fauna; people live by magic or magical items, growing food and raising cattle by force of will.

Abrams Rest

Sometimes called simply "The Rest." The only source of clean water in the Blighted West, and the reason its citizens keep coming — hoping for work and safety from the wasteland outside.

Liftdown

Sits over a gaping chasm at the centre of Abrams Rest — the access point to the Drop. Four bridges connect to a lift that carries miners from the surface down.

The Cobbles

The gated upper section of the city, northeast above the station. Entry is forbidden without a pass, guards man the gates, and it's the closest thing left to a normal fantasy town — an attempt to reclaim what things looked like before the Devastation. Home to Professor Godt's library. Coren once operated here, and stabbed Jareth in a Cobbles safehouse before fleeing the city.

The Colosseum

West side of Abrams Rest, run by the pitmaster Luddy. One of the city's only real entertainments — fights to the death, prisoners and animals held in cages, giant centipedes among them.

The Damn

Northwest section of the city (the misspelling is intentional). Source of the Baron's fresh water; he watches the Colosseum from its walls.

The Dancing Cat

A tavern in Lower Abrams Rest, near the Hexes. Miners gather here waiting on word of work from Ressa Vann.

The Darklands

A world within a world — a domain deep under the earth. Mining in the Drop is drawing steadily closer to it.

The Desert Rose

The famous pre-Devastation train the Baron discovered forty years ago, deep in the Drop.

The Drop

A vast, endless cavern beneath the Rest, where ancient ruins and Old World technology can still be found.

The Hexes

Market in Lower Abrams Rest, southeast, near the Slums and below the station. No weapons sold here — tools only, like knives.

The Library

Professor Godt's rumoured laboratory in the Cobbles. Looks more like a mansion than a civic building — ornate glass, bronze doors, built to resemble something ancient, wired with electricity likely drawn from magicite. A control panel sits beside the doors.

The Rows

Southwest section of the city, just south of the Colosseum.

The Station

East section of the city, just beneath the Cobbles — where arrivals on the Desert Rose first set foot in Abrams Rest.

The Slums

Southeast, behind the Hexes.

The Works

Southerly district; home to the Dancing Cat.

Inis Town

A coal and mining town in the Blighted West that supplies the rest of the region.

Pem

A village in the Blighted West — the suggested destination for finding magicite back in Session 1.

Rabbit Rock Mountains

Location of the goblin camp the party scouted to recover the Desert Rose's stolen magicite core.

Nar-Voth

Located in the Darklands. The Red Rider's likely home, and the domain he warned Abrams Rest not to enter again.

The Riad

A Roman-style bathhouse in the Cobbles; usage is a reward for loyalty to the Baron.

The Galleries

Market in the Cobbles where the city's best-quality goods are kept for sale.

The Magic Carpet

A theatre tavern located in the Cobbles.

Larriot's Little Luxuries

A stall in the Galleries — Quercucoonie sold his aquamarine here for 55 gold.

Factions

The Silent Knives

A resistance group opposing Baron Maxim Von Basten, based in Abrams Rest. Attempted a coup d'état five years ago that failed; the Baron's forces wiped the group out almost entirely.

Abrams Unrest

The name the party fights under — assumed only for their Colosseum match, so Lira could win back Thallion. Not the name they're otherwise known by.

Races

Elves

An ancient, slender, long-lived people from another world. Most are said to have returned there.

Goblins

Small, green-skinned, chaotic, oversized heads and razor teeth — vicious, voracious scavengers dwelling on society's fringes.

Humans

The most numerous people of the Blighted West, and the bulk of Abrams Rest's population — miners, guards, merchants, and everyone the Baron's quotas fall hardest on. No trait unites them beyond sheer numbers and a willingness to gamble on the Baron's promise of work and water.

Half-Elves

Born of human and elven blood. Foxfire, the mage Hylanku has spent years searching for, is half-elven.

Minotaurs

Large, bovine-headed, bestial humanoids.

Tengu

Avian humanoids resembling crows or ravens.

Tenuki

A raccoon-like race.

Venarans

An ape-like race.

Fauna

Giant Bats

Creatures seemingly from the Darklands. Attacked Abrams Rest and the Colosseum at the end of Session 5.

Giant Centipedes

Held in cages at the Colosseum.

Rhizan

Lizard-like creatures, cooked as street meat and sold in the Hexes.

Rock Rabbits

Rabbit-like creatures. Chango used their trail to track the goblins who stole the magicite core.

Zinza

A flocking animal, mentioned in the journal entry for Experiment Subject 126.

Key Items & Old World Relics

Aiudara

Teleportation gates once used by the elves to leave the Astlands and return to their home planet. All those found so far are depowered; the party encountered one in Session 4.

Magicite

A rare ore fused with magic, capable of powering machines and technology both old and new — the most sought-after resource in the land besides water. The Drop is one of the only places it's found in abundance, which is why the Baron drives Abrams Rest's people to mine it and to scavenge the Drop for Old World technology.

Setantii

A metal golem powered by magicite.
