Sessions

Ten sessions, one train wreck of a beginning, and a hole in the library floor.

Session 01

Six Goblins and a Dead Train

The party boarded the Desert Rose together as strangers. Six goblins crashed through the carriage windows; Verdant Shade obliterated one with a single punch, Lira and Luxem burned down more with magic, and Chango worked the rest with his pistols. Afterward Lira treated Luxem's wounds with mud, accepted begrudgingly.

The train's engineer, Thistogg, reached them once the fighting stopped and delivered the damage: every other passenger was dead, and the goblins had made off with the magicite core — the train wasn't going anywhere without it. He offered three options: strike out thirty miles west to Pem, head northeast toward a temple that might hold raw magicite, or track the goblins to their camp. Nathan Indiana Croft, Chango's prisoner, pushed for the temple and the treasure rumoured to be there. The party voted to go after the goblins instead.

Session 02

What Killed the Goblin Camp

Chango tracked ten miles east before sundown; half-eaten rock rabbits pointed the party toward the Rabbit Rock Mountains. Camp that night loosened tongues: Quercucoonie explained his dying homeland, Coren admitted he was chasing a rumour, Lira said only that she'd lost something important, and Hylanku said something that seemed to mean the same to him — though only Lira seemed to catch it. Nathan pushed Chango on whether he really meant to hand him to the Baron, and warned the party against showing magic openly in Abrams Rest.

The next day they found Jane — a woman drinking hard by a dead campfire, whose husband and son had been killed by the Baron's men hunting mages. Lira got through to her. Then they reached the goblin camp: already wiped out, every goblin dead, and a stone statue among the wreckage that woke up and attacked the instant they got close, knocking Luxem unconscious before Coren and Hylanku beat it back.

Session 03

Prying Out the Core

Nathan talked his way out of his restraints again, this time to explain how to safely strip the magicite from the construct's chest without setting it off. Chango agreed. Several attempts at extraction failed before Quercucoonie finally pulled it free. The camp itself had little worth taking, though the party refilled their waterskins and Lira and Hylanku freed a cage of raptors before heading back toward the train, Hylanku carrying the still-unconscious Luxem.

Session 04

The Elf Gate and the Setantii

Nathan named the animated construct a Setantii — an Old World machine — and filled in more of what he knew about the world before the Devastation, including the Baron's obsession with the ancient technology said to be buried in the Drop beneath Abrams Rest. The party found a carved archway wedged in a crevice: an Elf Gate, an Aiudara, once used by elves to return to their home planet, now sitting depowered like all the rest.

Four skeletons and a skeleton captain jumped the party next. Chango, Coren, Lira, and Hylanku cut through them, with Hylanku taking the worst of it to keep the others clear. That night, on watch, Coren started hearing whispers he didn't explain to anyone.

Session 05
In the Field
Liverpool

Arrival, and the Bats Over the Colosseum

Back aboard the repaired Desert Rose, Chango found an unmarked pocket watch engraved "T.R." No one recognised the initials. Nathan repeated his warning about magic in Abrams Rest — the Baron enslaves anyone who shows it — as the train pulled in past refugees begging at the gates.

The escort Phil Philliamson, "Crosswind Phil," met them at the Hexes and steered them toward the Dancing Cat. There, the barman Pok had heard nothing of a captured mage, and the foreman Ressa Vann told Coren the Silent Knives had been wiped out since the failed coup — she'd assumed he'd betrayed them all. Ressa hadn't heard that Jareth was dead, which meant he might not be. Pok pointed Coren toward the Cobbles library as the Professor's likely laboratory.

The next day, Quercucoonie visited a Venaran blacksmith trapped in debt to the Baron; Chango tried and failed to interest him in a rescue. Phil admitted, carefully, that he wanted the Baron gone — without saying who was paying him to babysit the party. Coren scouted the Cobbles gate guards and asked Ressa about passes.

That evening, fighting under the name Abrams Unrest, the party entered the Colosseum to win back Lira's captive elk, Thallion, marked for delivery to the Professor. They beat five returning champions — Phil alone killed three, including an orc that had knocked Hylanku down — and Quercucoonie bought them the win with a crowd-distracting victory speech while Lira revived Hylanku. Thallion was released and ran straight to Lira.

Then the sky opened. Giant bats tore into the arena and the crowd. Chango and Quercucoonie both went down; a red-skinned rider on a white bat screamed a warning about an invaded domain. The party fought a retreat through two gates — Coren holding one, Phil the other, shooting a bat out of the sky before he fled with the rest — and made it back to the Dancing Cat with everyone alive.

Session 06

The Masked Healer, and Ressa's Warning

The party regrouped inside the Dancing Cat, where a masked, beaked healer worked without a runic collar — and without explanation — to patch everyone up. Ressa, questioning where the bats had come from, guessed the Drop; Hylanku argued for the Darklands instead, and Coren confirmed the creatures had been dragging prisoners down with them.

Ressa handed over vague work permits and a single Cobbles gate pass. Coren, drinking with her, told her more of the truth about the night Jareth disappeared — though not all of it — and got a clear warning back: if she ever learned he'd had a hand in the Silent Knives' massacre, she'd kill him. He told her that was fair.

Outside, the white-bat rider called down a second warning: "Take this as warning, do not enter Nar-Voth again." Lira, raised on stories of the wider world, recognised the name as a region of the Darklands. Hylanku prayed to Jarak that night and received the blessed one devotion in answer — healing magic, and a deepened well of focus. The masked doctor admitted he had no idea why Luxem still wasn't waking up. Before they slept, the party invited Nathan along to the Cobbles; Lira cast Pet Cache and Thallion vanished into her hat, leaving antlers on the brim where he'd disappeared. Stepping back outside, they found a city torn open — bodies in the streets, stalls looted, and not a single dead guard among the wreckage.

Session 07

Into the Cobbles

Talked past the gate guards on the strength of Ressa's name, the party was handed to a guard named Gaskin and put to work — clearing bodies from a damaged garden, where Hylanku spotted a black-haired mage in a rusted collar mending a tree by hand. She told him the mages lived scattered near the Damn and the houses northwest of the library, and that she no longer had a name of her own to give him.

Coren lifted a pass off a dead nobleman while working. Offered a choice of further labour, the party took the Riad — a bathhouse whose defence protocol had locked down around a bat still loose inside. There they met Selene Von Basten, the Baron's daughter, who threatened the guards and eyed Hylanku and Quercucoonie with open menace before leaving them to it. Inside, five animated clay gargoyles attacked; the party dismantled them, and Verdant Shade tore an aquamarine eye from the last one standing.

Session 08

The Riad, and the Safehouse

Deeper in the bathhouse, the party found dead bodies in boiling pools, a pair of pristine white gloves that turned out to be magical, and a network of maintenance tunnels built for the gargoyles. Coren found a locked door with two more bodies and a potion of water breathing; another held a huddled family and the last bat, which Hylanku killed after Coren, Quercucoonie, and Chango wore it down. The rescued family handed over their Cobbles passes — four now, total.

The bat's severed head, thrown at the guards' feet, lifted the lockdown. In reward for the train, the Colosseum, and the Riad, the guards gave the party a flat on the Cobbles' third floor — the same safehouse Coren once shared with Jareth, and the same room where he'd stabbed him mid-spell before fleeing the city. A hidden drawer held a charcoal drawing of Coren; the entry hall still bore the faint scar of Jareth's old magic circle. That night Coren tried to scout the library alone, took a guard's bullet for his trouble, and made it back to decide scouting was better done as a group.

Session 09

Auctions, Funerals, and a Name for Foxfire

Abrams Rest tried to right itself. At the Galleries market, Quercucoonie sold the aquamarine gem for fifty-five gold and, on impulse, won a slave auction for a man listed only as "Auction Item 297." Renamed Little Freeman and then given back his real name — Kuldosk — he agreed to work off his own price rather than stay owned, and joined the party for dinner still muttering about it.

Lira spent the day helping wrap the dead in a makeshift church, quietly turning grief into distrust of the guards who'd let it happen. Hylanku traced rumours of a red-haired slave named Foxfire to an auction and a buyer close to the Baron — brown eyes, buzzed hair, never without a cigarette — then visited the blacksmith Yangu, who broke Hylanku's manacle chain but left his wristbands on as a reminder. At the Dancing Cat, Ressa delivered the worst news of the session: Luxem had died in her sleep, no wound, no clear cause. Hylanku prayed over her, took her bottle of Iron Wine, and arranged for her to be included in the city's mass funeral rites.

Coren, working the Galleries for anyone who remembered the coup, found a cobbler who confirmed it: Lorcend Von Basten, the Baron's silver-haired son, had personally redirected Jareth's body toward the library. Coren scouted the building in daylight, climbed to an open window, and broke its latch so it couldn't be shut again.

Session 10

Hylanku's Story, and the Floor Gives Way

Over drinks at the Magic Carpet, the party traded the day's news and Hylanku finally told his backstory: raised by the wandering caravan that took him in half-feral from the Coccium Ruins, taught the ways of Jarak by the minotaur elder Old Bull, bound in friendship to the half-elf caster Foxfire — until the Baron's men came for her, Old Bull died protecting the caravan at the hands of a silver-haired enforcer, and Hylanku was dragged into the mines around Inis Town, where he proved himself finding Magicite deep underground before being sent on to Abrams Rest.

After dark, the party climbed back through Coren's broken window into the library with Kuldosk in tow. Inside, they split up: Quercucoonie found scholarship suggesting the Tenuki cast a mass ritual that held back the Devastation in his homeland, without the spell itself recorded. Coren, Lira, Chango, and Hylanku turned up a pedestal with a nine-number lock, and journals for Subjects 001, 126, and 442 — the last one, unmistakably, about Jareth.

A gelatinous, half-melted creature that could only say "Ort" hid from them and flinched from Lira's offer to help. A locked room held a chained statue and candles that unsettled Quercucoonie for reasons tied to his own god, Orth, plus a vial of adder's poison and a medallion marking the Horns of Bos — a minotaur royal guard order long since scattered. Cross-referencing the successful experiment numbers gave the party their combination: 126, 442, 469. They entered it. The floor opened beneath them onto a stairwell no one had built for guests.
