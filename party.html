Main Party

Chango K Caza

Gunslinger · Venaran

A Venaran bounty hunter who boarded the Desert Rose with his prisoner, the archaeologist Nathan Indiana Croft, in tow. He's since loosened Nathan's bindings in exchange for what he knew about stopping the Setantii — whether he means to let the man go entirely, he still hasn't said. He still carries an unmarked pocket watch engraved "T.R.", found on that same train, that no one has been able to place.

Off the clock, he wants the indentured Venaran blacksmith freed, and a way of his own up into the Cobbles. Keeps to himself more than most — cleaning his pistols, trading quiet words with Nathan — but the Colosseum attack rattled him enough to admit, afterward, that he needed a break.

In Session 12, a man matching the Professor's description greeted Chango by name, like they already knew each other. In Session 13, he accepted the Professor's offer before anyone else could: deputy badges for the whole party, in exchange for clearing a vault belonging to Marhesh Dar, the Mad Jester King. Only then did he learn why the Professor knew him: he claims to have given Chango sentience, long ago, purely for his own entertainment. Chango remembered only fragments, and a word he couldn't stop screaming: "Papa."

In Session 14, he withdrew to his room without a word and has not come out since. Lira is worried enough to wonder whether Yangu might reach him when the rest of them can't.

Coren Vael

Rogue · Human (Changeling)

One blue eye, one green — a mark of his birth lineage — and once a member of the Silent Knives. He fled Abrams Rest after a botched solo job, stabbing his partner Jareth mid-spell in their safehouse before running for reasons he still hasn't fully explained.

Ressa told him what he came back to: the Knives wiped out in his absence, herself spared and put to work as a foreman. A journal on Subject 442 finally answered what happened to Jareth's body — and revealed that Coren's mother is the hag Callow May, a secret he's at last shared with the party. Born in Dreadmere Marsh; these days, home is nowhere in particular.

In Session 13, he turned the Professor's conversation toward Jareth, then toward his mother. The Professor relayed a message from her, "Dinner will always be on the table," and Coren told him she could choke on it. Asked to let Jareth die, the Professor refused: Callow May had seen to it that not even he could grant that anymore. Coren left with a quiet vow: "I will end this." Back at the flat, he told the party the whole truth: a changeling sent away by a terrified father, and a blade meant to cut Callow May's grip from Jareth, not end him. He still wears Jareth's ring.

In Session 14, he drove a dagger through the charcoal drawing Jareth once made of him, then asked Ressa to help revive the Silent Knives. "I want to tear it all down." She told him to count her out, but left him a private lounge at the Dancing Cat to wait in. He has reached Level 3.

Hylanku Lonehorn

Champion · Minotaur

His left horn was shattered by the same bullet that killed Old Bull, the minotaur who found him feral and broken at the Coccium Ruins and raised him after. He speaks in blunt, almost neanderthal common, and carries real guilt over failing to protect the party during the bat attack at the Colosseum. A prayer to Jarák afterward answered with the blessed one devotion and a deeper well of healing magic.

In Session 10, he finally told the party the whole story: Old Bull died shielding Foxfire, a red-haired mage who'd kept their caravan fed, from the Baron's men — and the silver-haired enforcer who killed him and broke Hylanku's horn was confirmed, that same session, as Lorcend Von Basten. Hylanku was dragged into the mines before learning whether Foxfire got away, and hasn't stopped looking.

Beneath the Professor's library, he recognised Old Bull's bones built into a guardian construct, and in Session 13 learned the Professor had used them for "another experiment" since. He answered by hurling a slab of stone at the Professor, which shattered without leaving a mark. He also learned that the Horns of Bos amulet belonged to the First Horn of the old Minotaur Empire, and that Old Bull's axe is still out there, just not with the Professor.

In Session 14, he wondered why Old Bull ever carried that emblem, then reached for Jarak and felt the god straining to answer him. He is certain now that Jarak is real, and that he may be the old god's last hope. He reached Level 3, watches over Lira, and, warned by Pok not to trust the Baron's badges, leaves his own unworn. He still wears Yangu's half plate and his manacle wristbands.

Lira Cervan

Druid · Elf

Came to Abrams Rest looking for her elk, Thallion, and enlisted the party to help win him back in a Colosseum fight — successfully. She keeps him tucked inside her hat when he's not needed; antlers appear on the brim the moment he vanishes into it.

Spent a day helping wrap the bat attack's dead for the city's funeral rites, and used the grief in the room to plant a little distrust of the guards who'd let it happen. Tries reading the future by dust and jar more often than it actually works. Home is the Dead Woods, whenever she makes it back.

Beneath the Professor's library, it was her hand that found the hidden catch behind a false book spine, and her senses that first caught something "twisted," not cursed, waiting further in. In the tank chamber a diseased festrog dropped her twice, and a wave of green smoke she couldn't control swept the room clean.

In Session 13, she confirmed the Professor was no illusion, and found the magic layered onto him almost too much to take in. She warned the others he might still be watching, and set the party's course: rest, gather materials, then plan. In Session 14, she slept curled against Thallion, feeling stronger and more resilient after nearly dying, and has reached Level 3. She wrapped Luxem in linen and sang her an elven farewell. Asked whether she'd work for the Professor to get closer to the Baron, and so closer to helping Thallion, she said only that she'd be there when needed. She wants time to study first.

Luxem

Sorcerer · Elf

Never said why she was travelling to Abrams Rest, and wasn't glad to find another elf already in the party. She unwittingly triggered the Setantii at the goblin camp and was knocked cold — and never fully woke from it, a mystery even the Plague Doctor couldn't explain despite everything looking physically fine.

Died in her sleep during the Cobbles work; Hylanku learned of it in Session 9. In Session 14, Lira and Hylanku wrapped her in white linen, Lira sang her an elven farewell, and Thallion carried her to the Church of Abram. The church's elder laid her on a shelf for the dead, and Quercucoonie placed a desert rose from Verdant Shade on her.

Quercucoonie

Summoner · Tenuki

On a mission to reforest his dying homeland, and to understand why it's dying in the first place. He's asked Ressa about survey work involving the mind, and prays to a deity named Orth. On impulse, bought a man being sold at auction as "Item 297," meant to free him outright, and ended up with Kuldosk joining the party instead, until Session 13, when Kuldosk, having seen enough for one lifetime, left for good. Quercucoonie was sadder about it than he expected.

In Session 13, Ort gave him a small book bound in the symbol of Orf, a different god to his own. Told as a limerick, it explained how Orf once helped the Tenuki protect the Lang Tree from the Great Devastation with a spell bound to an artefact. That protection is failing now, and the book explains why his homeland stopped regenerating. In Session 14, he found hope in it: if magic protected the forest once, perhaps it can be restored. He turned himself into a crowbar to pry open the jammed flat door, wears his deputy's badge openly, and laid a desert rose from Verdant Shade on Luxem at the Church of Abram.

His summoned eidolon, Verdant Shade — a tree-like creature that shares his health pool — rarely strays far, and reforms from roots when it's dismissed by force. Home is the Hodenbaumhaut Forest, the place he's trying to save.

FAMILIARS & SUMMONS

Thallion

Elk · Lira's familiar

The elk Lira came to Abrams Rest looking for. Found captive beneath the Colosseum, marked for delivery to Professor Godt — the party won him back by fighting under the name Abrams Unrest, and he ran straight to her the moment he was released.

Lira can tuck him away with a spell called Pet Cache; he leaps into her hat and vanishes, leaving antlers on the brim until he's called back out. He's carried wounded party members to safety more than once, including through the bat attack's retreat from the Colosseum.

Verdant Shade

Eidolon · Quercucoonie's summon

A tree-like creature summoned by Quercucoonie, and the first thing the party ever saw him do — obliterating a goblin with a single punch aboard the Desert Rose in Session 1. Shares his health pool, and vanishes if he's knocked unconscious, only to reform later from a twisting mass of roots.

Tore an aquamarine eye from a defeated homunculus in the Riad, and threw Quercucoonie bodily up through the library window in Session 10 while he was in smaller animal form. Rarely strays far from him.

THE ROSTER

Temporarily Joined the Party

Phil Philliamson

Gunslinger · Human

Joined the party the moment they arrived — a benefactor paid him to watch them, though he's never said who. Nicknamed "Crosswind Phil," he hinted there are ways into the Cobbles that don't need a pass.

An old injury left him scarred and imbued with magic that once saved his life; the scar still flashes blue and whispers at the mention of magic. He wants the Baron gone, fought hard in the Colosseum, and was last seen fleeing the opposite gate once the bats came down.

THE ONE BEHIND THE CURTAIN

Karl

Dungeon Master

Never seen in Abrams Rest, and yet nothing happens there without him. Every quota, every closed gate, every bat that falls out of a clear sky answers to him first. He built the Blighted West grain by grain, gave the Baron his cruelty and the Professor his curiosity, and has never once let the party see the whole map.

Roll well, and he might let you keep something you love. Roll badly, and — by the look on his face — he already knew you would.
