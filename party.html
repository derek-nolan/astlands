The Party

Six strangers who boarded a train together. They fight under a borrowed name now.

Main Party

Chango K Caza

Player: Jonny · Gunslinger · Venaran
  • Bounty hunter who boarded the Desert Rose with Nathan Indiana Croft as his prisoner.
  • Eventually removed Nathan's bindings after Nathan shared information on stopping the Setantii — hasn't said whether he intends to free him outright.
  • Wants to free the indentured Venaran blacksmith of Abrams Rest.
  • Looking for a way up to the Cobbles.

Coren Vael

Player: Derek · Rogue · Human
  • Heterochromic eyes — one blue, one green — marking his birth lineage. Member of the Silent Knives.
  • Fled Abrams Rest after a botched solo assassination job, stabbing his partner Jareth in the safehouse before running for reasons still unshared.
  • Learned from Ressa that the Silent Knives were wiped out in his absence, and that she now works as a foreman for the Baron.
  • A journal entry (Subject 442) revealed what happened to Jareth's body — and that Coren's mother is the hag Callow May, which he's finally shared with the party.
  • Home: born in Dreadmere Marsh. Currently no fixed location.
