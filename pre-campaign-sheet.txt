Lore 
* The Great Devastation 
   * A thousand years ago, a great calamity befell the world. What specifically happened, mortal kind no longer remembers, but the effects remain to this day. 
* Magic
   * People who display magical tendencies in The Blighted West may be captured in order to produce crops, water and food and other services. Due to this it is normal to hide any magical abilities to avoid being captured. 


* The Old World
   * The name given to the world pre Great Devastation. 


* Slavery
   * There is slavery in The Blighted West, particularly the enslavement of people with magical abilities. These people are captured and used to magically produce crops, water and food.
* Slave Collars
   * Runic collars are used to suppress the abilities of magic users, all magic is suppressed when wearing a collar except for whichever spell aligns to the runes on the collar. 


* Water 
   * Most naturally occurring water is acidic and toxic to most life. Even the oceans surrounding the Blighted West are acidic. 


* Funeral Rites
   * Bodies are wrapped in cloth and then placed on the water, then float to the waterfall, falling into the drop, this has been the ritual follows by the people of Abrams Rest for decades. 
* Professor Journal Entries from Session 10 
Subject 001
Day 1 - The Idea
It's been two centuries now, and the sense of boredom I am facing is beginning to take its toll. So, I have decided to learn a thing or two in the ways of science. Coupled with my magick, I believe I will go far in this field, and to start with I would like a servant.
Day 9 - Subject 001
I have found a decent candidate, a young woman I have been courting these last few months, and she is unaware of my nature. I have been reading the works of a scientist called Haber who uses chemicals and biological viruses to manifest physiological change in a subject. I am gong to use the ideas of his studies together with my arcane knowledge to see if I can create a being of subservience.
Day 27 - Halfway through
She is coming along quite well. The change occurring is causing her immense pain, she looks at me and begs for me to end her life. I haven't been so amused in such a long time. I will note that her skin is starting to peel, I am hoping that it is some sort of shedding while her new body is taking shape.
Day 46 - Transformation complete
She has survived her transformation and sufficed to say, it was not what I was hoping for. I wanted an able bodied husk to serve me! Not some horrific blob that slathers and wails in pain most of the day. Not to matter, as first attempts go this isn't a bad one. One surprising revelation is that no matter how much damage and trauma I inflict upon it, it heals almost instantaneously! Oh I'm going to have such fun.


Experiment Notes - Subject 126 (suspected to be Chango)
Day 1 – Subject 126
Following the untimely demise of the mamlambo, I have acquired a new specimen.
A young male ape taken from the banks of the Nanji River. An unintelligent, crude cousin to the equally disdainful Vanarans who roam the surrounding area of the river in their caravans. I saw this one use a heavy rock to take down a lone zinza that had seemingly lost its flock. He threw it with such...intention. I put him to sleep while he happily ate his prize.
He is currently in a rage trying to break the glass of his tank with his fists. I shall wait until he tires, then begin.
Day 18 – three doses weekly.
The subject has begun his metamorphosis. I placed a rudimentary puzzle in his cage and asked him to complete it. It would normally take an ape the better part of three hours to complete such a task. It took the subject 30 seconds. Very promising.
His verbal comprehension is also coming on leaps and bounds. Though not speaking, he is beginning to understand complete sentences, questions and instructions that would usually befuddle such a creature. Starting tomorrow, I will begin upping his dosage to move the process along.
Day 57 – two doses daily.
His motor skills have improved drastically. He is now able to hold a knife with purpose, and use it to strike accordingly. The serum appears to be altering his body in exactly the way I hoped. His spine has now grown more like a bipedal, and coupling this with his animal physicality, he will make a ferocious warrior. The biological changes seem to begin while he sleeps, must study this further. Perhaps a trigger is the relaxation of slumber? I have resorted to casting a silence spell around his cage at night.
I have grown quite fond of this subject, he has ceased to look at me filled with rage, and now seems to understand the gifts I am giving him. He seems determined. Having said this, I noticed him counting the exits from the laboratory. How quaint, I may indulge him one day and see how he does. It would be fascinating live data on his abilities.
Day 237 – Termination of experiment.
The mages have finally found what they're looking for, and soon this world will end. I have placed the subject in cryogenic stasis, and I hope he will remain until I return. I warned that fool Abram, and now I must clean up his mess.
Before I leave, I should give him a name...


Experiment notes Subject 227
Day 1 -  Another attempt with the virus. I have yet to find another subject to take to the virus as well as subject 126, but I will keep trying. I will need a vessel when the time comes.
So the latest subject is a gnome female, presumably at the start of her adult life. I am hoping youthful vigour and a level of intelligence will help me further my pursuits. It has been hard since what folks are now calling "The Great Devastation" to procure subjects I feel are suited to my experiments, but beggars can't be choosers.
Day 25 - Two doses dailyI have decided to speed up my experiment, in case brute force will work in this case, and that the subject isn't showing any signs of improvement. All that wailing and nothing to show for it. Pathetic.

Experiment Notes - Subject 442 (relates to Jareth’s body and Coren's mother)
Day 1 - The morning after the arrests.
 The Baron has successfully put down the coup d'état before it had even began. How droll, even after a thousand years mortals still manage to play out the same performances over and over again.Though something rather interesting occurred last night. I felt a surge of magick coming directly from Resident Square. Specifically it felt like crone magic. Haven't felt that in a very long time. I've asked Lorcend to investigate and bring me anything they may find, including people and corpses.
Day 2 - Subject 442
Well isn't this a delight! A young man, stabbed through the neck, yet he still breathes! I've stabilised him for now, but the magick keeping him alive is definitely hag. Oh my poor boy, what did you do for one of those creatures to show you their contempt? It's been such a long time since I've met anyone who is as powerful as me...almost. I wonder if I could make contact.
Day 21 - Contact made. 
I finally made contact with the hag. A green hag to be precise, going by the name Callow May. She says the boy was the lover of her son, and well, she couldn't be having that. She has cursed the boy to never die, but to never heal. She says I may do what I please with him, but she will not lift the curse. Nor would I want that! I had the most extraordinary idea, and she confirmed her curse would aid in this endeavour. She also asked me to keep her informed if anyone came looking for this boy. I gave my word.
Day 63 - Piece by piece. 
I am in heaven. I am able to remove immensely large pieces of the subject and replace them with parts from much larger, stronger creatures. I was also finally able to use the dragon hide I'd been saving too. He has also been the only other subject to and this boy will become the ultimate weapon, and may be a replacement vessel that I have been looking for after all this time!
Subject 469 (relates to Old Bull)
Day 1 - Subject 469
Lorcend has brought me a cadaver "to play with"...his words. It is an elderly minotaur male. While minotaurs are extremely rare nowadays (I don't think I've seen one since their Empire's fall) I have hardly any use for an aged corpse.
Though I have read somewhere that the bones of one of this disgusting beasts are as strong as iron, even in old age. Maybe it will have some use.
Day 23 - Beginning the ritual
I have decided to use this subject after all, it will make a fine guardian to my home. I just need to gather some ingredients: the root of a corpse flower, a small animal (alive), and a flawless obsidian gemstone to bind the magicks. The gemstone will be the hardest to find, but oh if it isn't fun to do this again! I'll send men out into The Blight, I seem to recall a mine somewhere in the region from before. Surely it must still be there!
Day 39 - Ingredients found
I now have all the ingredients I need to begin the ritual. I asked Lorcend since I decided to do this to bring me the minotaurs belongings. He had nothing particular of note, apart from the amulet belonging to The Horns of the Bos, the royal guard of the Minotaur Emperor, and a war axe.
The amulet is certainly unusual. The Minotaur Empire was lost to The Night Plains, why would this one carry it around? No matter, with this axe he will make a fine guard.
Day 42 - A new guard
I had to discard most of the flesh and solely use his skeleton, but my is he strong! And listens to every command I give him without question, nothing of his soul remains now. I took some of the muscle and a horn for my prized subject, oh he will look incredible!

Sessions
Session 1 


The party boarded the train to Abram’s Rest together. Six goblins attacked the carriage by crashing through the windows. The party fought off the goblin attack. Quercucoonie's eidolon, Verdantshade, obliterated a goblin with one punch. Lira and Luxem used their magic to take out goblins while chango shot goblins with his pistols. After the fight, Lira used mud to treat Luxem's injuries, which she begrudgingly accepted. Thistogg the train engineer reached the party after the attack. He told them that all the other passengers on the train were dead. He also explained that the goblins had stolen the train’s magicite core. The party learned the train could not continue without a replacement core. They were given options for what to do next: 
* Travel to Pem, 30 miles west, 
* Go northeast to a temple where raw magicite might be found
* Track the goblins to their camp
Nathan Indiana Croft, Chango’s prisoner, suggested the temple as an option and mentioned possible treasure there. The party decided to go after the goblins.
Session 2

Several years prior to our campaign:
A roving caravan in the Blighted West, consisting of various races, religions, creeds etc, seeking survival by travelling and living together - among them a young red-haired half-elven caster who goes by the name Foxfire, seeks to avoid the “indentured servitude” offered by the Baron’s regime by providing her services to the caravan. Also among their number, a wizened old minotaur “shaman-esque” figure they refer to simply as Old Bull, who is a devout follower of Jarak, the 6-horned god, the first Minotaur. Old Bull appears to be extremely in tune with the nature/spirits of Earth (Petran) and acts as a guide for where the caravan should travel, proving adept at avoiding trouble, which behooves (lol) a caravan lacking in martial strength.
Seeking refuge from the arid wasteland of the Blighted West, and perhaps drawn by some spiritual gut feeling, Old Bull guides the caravan to the Coccium Ruins.  After some time spent exploring and finding what little of value they can, the Caravan come across a huge, but emaciated young Minotaur (Hylanku) who seems barely sentient, half-mad and lashing out at their approach. Where the caravan largely are content to leave him to it, having enough problems of their own, Old Bull approaches, calming Hylanku with his spiritual presence and the fact he is another minotaur (Hylanku has no memory of ever encountering his own kind before), he eventually manages to talk to the young Minotaur and convince the Caravan (who are inclined to listen to Old Bull) of the merit in bringing Hylanku along.
Unsure of his time before the Coccium ruins, (Hylanku does not remember anything prior to the ruins). He is unsure how long he wandered the ruins, mindless and barely surviving on whatever he could find to sustain himself.
Over the next several years Hylanku travels with the caravan, earning his keep with his intimidating presence that helps keep minor bandits and rival caravans from causing any trouble. During this time Hylanku learns at the feet of Old Bull, becoming in tune with the innate earth (Petran) nature of his kind (Slabsoul). Old Bull teaches him of the first Minotaur, Jarak and legends of Minotaur past etc. Hylanku grows big and strong, forming fast friendship and mutual trust with Foxfire and finds a role as a protector to this caravan who took him in and gave him a life.
Part of how the caravan makes a life for themselves is trading the food/water provided by Foxfire. Eventually, the Baron’s men get word of a caster providing food/water in the Blighted West and come knocking. They attack the caravan, looking to enslave Foxfire, Old Bull stands in their way while Hylanku tries to protect the caravan and hide Foxfire. Old Bull is beaten down to his knees but still refusing to give up Foxfire, fed up with his resistance, a Baron’s enforcer with long silver hair and a scar over his left eye draws a rifle on Old Bull.
Hylanku tries to intervene to protect Old Bull, who is angry that Hylanku hasn’t taken the caravan (Foxfire) away to safety. The silver haired enforcer shoots at Old Bull, Hylanku tries to get in the way, the bullet shattering his left horn and striking Old Bull in the chest. Hylanku watches Old Bull die while he is manacled and taken to be enslaved, the Baron’s men figuring a hulking minotaur will be an asset in the mines, he never sees if the Baron’s men capture Foxfire as he is dragged off. The only possession Hylanku retains is an ancient emblem, which Old Bull told him was from the before time and shows the symbol of the 6 horned god which Old Bull bestowed upon Hylanku after a particularly meaningful day of his teachings. Hylanku manages to hide this in his furs throughout his time as a slave. Vowing to avenge Old Bull, Hylanku decides he will prove himself an asset, in order to stay alive, should an opportunity arise.
Only a few months after being put to work in the mines surrounding Inis Town, it becomes clear quickly that Hylanku is extremely well cut out for mining work, able to wend his way deeper into the mines than his fellow slaves, able to stay out there for longer, carry more etc. Over the coming months Hylanku again takes on a protective role, keeping other slaves alive deep in the mines, by pointing out particularly dangerous areas, finding routes back and retrieving lost miners who would normally be left to die. His time in the mines leaves him scarred by the sharp ores of the mines - but toughened. His Prowess earns him some status amongst the slaves and with the Baron’s men, affording him a chainmail vest to protect him from potentially fatal injury when in the most treacherous parts of the mine.
Able to make his way deeper into the Inis Town mines than any before him, Hylanku ends up finding Magicite Ore deep, deep in the Inis Town mine, and feels a resonance coming from his Emblem of Jarak. Bringing Magicite back to the Baron’s men, they decide Hylanku should be brought to the Baron himself, to be used at Abram’s rest as a slave capable of exploring deep into The Drop.  - end of hylanku’s tale about his backstory
