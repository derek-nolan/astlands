First raised: Session 1

Last traced to the Cobbles slave auctions, bought by someone close to the Baron — short brown hair, brown eyes, always smoking. Hylanku hasn't stopped looking, and doesn't yet know if she's even still alive.

Nathan challenged Chango directly about handing him over to the Baron. Chango has never actually answered — Nathan's still travelling with the party, still technically a prisoner, and still doesn't know which way this ends.

Nathan has hinted more than once that he knows things useful to the Baron, and separately that he knows something about the Professor "from before his time." Neither has ever been explained.

Coren's Whispers

First raised: Session 4

On watch the night after the skeleton fight near the Aiudara, Coren began hearing whispers in the dark. He's never explained what they said, or whether they've come back since.

The Pocket Watch

Chango found an unmarked pocket watch engraved with the initials "T.R." aboard the Desert Rose. No one in the party has recognised them since.

Who Paid Phil

First raised: Session 5

Crosswind Phil admitted an unnamed benefactor paid him to watch the party, and separately that he wanted the Baron gone — but never who was behind either. He fled the Colosseum attack out the opposite gate and hasn't been seen since.

OpenPhil Philliamson

The Rider's Grudge

First raised: Session 5

The horned rider called Abrams Rest's presence an invasion of a domain, then warned the city off Nar-Voth by name. Whether that's connected to the Baron's mining pushing ever deeper into the Darklands is still just the party's best guess.

OpenRed RiderNar-Voth

How Is the Plague Doctor Free?

First raised: Session 6

Hylanku asked him directly how he uses magic openly without a collar or an owner. He gave no real answer. In a city that enslaves every mage it finds, that alone makes him worth watching.

OpenPlague Doctor

Selene's Interest

First raised: Session 7

The Baron's daughter made her interest in Hylanku and Quercucoonie clear at the Riad — half threat, half something else. Neither the threat nor the something else has come up again since.

OpenSelene Von Basten

Why Luxem Died

First raised: Session 9

The masked healer's best guess was a bleed within the brain — except ordinary magic should have caught and repaired something like that. No one has looked into it further since the funeral.

OpenLuxem

The Lost Tenuki Ritual

First raised: Session 10

A record in the Professor's library confirmed the Tenuki once cast a mass ritual that held the Blight back from their homeland before the Devastation — but not how. If the method can be recovered, Quercucoonie's whole reason for coming to Abrams Rest might finally have an answer.

OpenQuercucoonie

Who Is Subject 126?

First raised: Session 10

A journal in the Professor's library describes a young Venaran taken from the banks of the Nanji River, altered dose by dose, and placed in cryogenic stasis before the Great Devastation. The Professor never gave him a name. Whether that has anything to do with Chango's own past is nothing more than a shared river and a shared race so far — worth remembering, not worth assuming.

OpenSubject 126

Old Bull's Guardian

First raised: Session 10

The Professor's journal on Subject 469 describes stripping Old Bull's body to the bone and binding what was left with necromantic magic — a new guard, following every command with nothing of who he used to be left in it. The party has only read about it so far. They haven't met it.

OpenOld BullSubject 469

What Jareth Is Becoming

First raised: Session 10

The same journal that confirmed Jareth was alive also described what he's being turned into: pieces of him replaced with parts from larger creatures, dragon hide grafted on, the Professor calling him a possible "ultimate weapon" and a "replacement vessel." The party knows what's being done to him. They haven't seen what it's made him into.

OpenJarethSubject 442

The Creature Called "Ort"

First raised: Session 10

A half-melted, half-humanoid survivor of the Professor's experiments, found hiding in the library. It can — or will — only say one word. Whether it was once a gnome, and whether it can be helped at all, is still unknown.

OpenThe Library

What's Below the Library

First raised: Session 10

The combination lock gave way to a hidden stairway at the end of Session 10. The party hasn't set foot on it yet.
